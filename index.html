Where are we losing subscribers, and what is it costing?

Churn, retention, revenue risk and customer segments for a subscription business. Use the filters to slice the 15-customer sample; every chart and insight updates.

KPI definitions & risk-score rules
  • Churn rate = churned customers ÷ all customers in view.
  • Retention rate = 1 − churn rate.
  • MRR lost = sum of MonthlyCharges of churned customers. % of MRR = that ÷ MRR of all customers in view.
  • Revenue at risk = MonthlyCharges of active customers whose risk band is Medium or High (forward-looking). Annualised = ×12.
  • Tenure KPIs = average TenureMonths, compared for churned vs retained customers.
  • Risk score (0–10): contract (M2M 3 / 1-yr 1 / 2-yr 0) + tenure (≤6: 3, 7–12: 2, 13–24: 1, >24: 0) + tickets (≥5: 3, 3–4: 2, 1–2: 1, 0: 0) + Basic plan 1. High ≥ 7, Medium 4–6, Low ≤ 3.
Source: cleaned_customer_churn.csv · n = 15 customers. Small sample: patterns are indicative, not statistically conclusive. Colours are paired with hatching and text labels so meaning never relies on colour alone.
